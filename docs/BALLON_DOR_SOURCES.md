# Ballon d'Or boards: sources

Checked 4 October 2026. The boards are in `index.html` (search for `bdo-`), under Top 5 Leagues.

| Board | Source | Second check |
|---|---|---|
| `bdo-most-wins`: the ten players who have won it more than once (Messi 8, Ronaldo 5; Cruyff, Platini, Van Basten 3; Di Stefano, Beckenbauer, Keegan, Rummenigge, Ronaldo Nazario 2) | Wikipedia, "Ballon d'Or", wins by player | Exactly ten players have two or more wins, so there is no shared place. |
| `bdo-w-1993` (1993 to 2002) and `t5-bdo-2002` (2002 to 2011): the winner each year | `BDO` in `index.html` | Wikipedia's winners table. Other ten-year windows are left out: Messi or Ronaldo fill four or more slots in them. 2020 had no award. |
| `bdo-vote-2022` to `bdo-vote-2025`: the top ten in each vote, with points | Wikipedia's page for each year (the Ballon d'Or ranking table) | 2022: TNT Sports and Topend Sports (order and top points); 2023: 90min and Sportskeeda (all ten); 2024: 90min and Goal; 2025: Sports Illustrated and UEFA.com. Points for places 8 to 10 in 2022 are Wikipedia's; the order is confirmed by TNT. |

From 2022 the award covers a season (August to July), not a calendar year, so each vote board's date line names its season.

To add: the 2026 vote after the ceremony on 26 October 2026 (`bdo-vote-2026`, for 2025/26). With 2026, the winners from 2016 to 2026 also make a ten-award board (Messi on three, Ronaldo on two).
