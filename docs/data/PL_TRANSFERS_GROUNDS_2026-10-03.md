# Transfers, grounds and shirt numbers (researched 3 October 2026)

Fee basis for every fee board: the headline fee (guaranteed plus reported add-ons) as the most prominent UK outlets reported it, in pounds. Where the repo's existing fee boards (`scripts/build-pl-records.py`, `docs/data/PL_SALES_2026-10-02.md`, `docs/data/PL_SIGNINGS_2026-10-03.md`) already carry a figure, I have used the same figure so the boards agree, and say so. Fees run to the end of the summer 2026 window (1 September 2026). Everything else stops at the end of 2025/26.

How the data was gathered: Wikipedia wikitext (fetched directly), Transfermarkt, ESPN, Goal, SI, Football365, Planet Football, WhatCulture and footballgroundguide.com pages (fetched with curl), plus web search result text quoting Sky Sports, BBC, ITV, PA, TNT Sports, AP, Reuters and others.

## Flags

1. **Newcastle number 9 has 11 players.** The rule is exactly ten answers. Options: make it "since 1995" (drops Cole, leaving ten), or use a pool. Both sources agree on all eleven names.
2. **Biggest sales abroad, 10th place:** Diaz £65.5m and Rodri £65.4m are effectively level (both are euro conversions). I put Diaz 10th. A pool of the two for 10th is safer. Jhon Duran (£64.5m plus add-ons that were never published) is just below. If any outlet gives a total with add-ons for Duran, he could go above both.
3. **Hazard: £130m or £150m.** Some outlets (The Cable, citing UK reports) said up to £150m. At £150m he moves above Coutinho to 1st. The membership doesn't change. The repo already uses £130m.
4. **Bellingham £115m** (ITV/PA with add-ons). TNT's headline was £112m. Either way he is 3rd.
5. **Kane £100m** is the commonly reported figure for initial plus add-ons. The guaranteed figure is £86.4m, Bayern's Hoeness claimed about £82m, and Wikipedia notes up to £103m. Either way he stays in the top ten.
6. **Yoro: £58.9m against the repo's £59.9m.** ITV and Sky (August 2026) both say £58.9m (EUR 62m plus EUR 8m), and the repo's signings doc says "about £59m". The `pl-fee-def` board's "£59.9m" looks £1m too high. This doesn't change any placing.
7. **Quenda: £43.6m or £40m.** Sporting's statement (EUR 52m) converts to £43.6m. The 2026 signing reports, Wikipedia and the repo say £40m. At £40m he ties Gray and Ahanor, which still gives ten names.
8. **Teenagers who signed early:** Estevao (agreed aged 17, arrived aged 18) and Quenda (agreed aged 17, signed aged 19) are teenagers either way. Ahanor signed for Chelsea aged 18 but is on loan at Crystal Palace until 2027. Lewis Hall was 18 on the loan and 19 when it became permanent. All count under "aged 19 or under on the day".
9. **January 2026 teenagers were not fully checked.** A full list of January 2026 deals couldn't be fetched. Rayan (Bournemouth, £24.7m) was the only notable teenage signing found, and it is well short of the cut. The summer 2026 window was checked against ESPN's full club-by-club list.
10. **Attendances are not the Premier League's official figures.** premierleague.com couldn't be reached. I used Transfermarkt (full table, and its season total matches the match-by-match sum), checked against footballgroundguide and a third table. All three agree on the top ten. The only order difference is Everton and Newcastle at 7th and 8th (52,132 and 52,095 on Transfermarkt; 52,132 and 52,161 in the third table).
11. **Etihad capacity:** I used the handbook's 52,900, since the expansion opened only for the last game of 2025/26. On the current 61,038 the Etihad is 4th instead of 6th, with the same ten grounds.
12. **Single-source or soft figures:** Gordon £69.3m (Wikipedia's breakdown; Yahoo agrees; ESPN gives only £60.5m initial). Rodri £65.4m (ESPN £51m initial). Ahanor £40m (AP only). Mbaye £47m (Reuters/theScore; ESPN "undisclosed").
13. **Man Utd number 7 gaps:** 7 was unused in 2013/14 and from summer 2017 to January 2018. Cavani wore it into the opening weeks of 2021/22 before Ronaldo took it on 2 September 2021. Neither point changes the ten names.
