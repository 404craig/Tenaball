== README
Tenaball: collected EPL data
Collected 1 Oct 2026 for Tenaball. This is research data, not finished questions.
Instructions for Claude Code (TENEBALL chat)
This is collected data from Craig. Analyse it and incorporate it into new Tenaball questions however you see fit.
Follow the existing Tenaball rules: every question states its period as a line under the question; no answer in 4 or more slots; PL player/goal data counts from 1992/93.
Where several entries tie at the bottom of a list, every tied entry is included so any of them can count as a valid answer. Tied entries at the cut-off are highlighted yellow.
Rows marked 'Next' or 'Just outside' are reserves and are NOT valid answers to a top-10 question.
Data currency
2025/26 is the last complete season. 2026/27 is five matchweeks in; the next round is 10 to 12 Oct 2026.
Live lists that can change: manager wins, points and win % (Moyes, Arteta, Maresca active), hat-tricks, Welbeck's seasons count.
Guardiola left Man City in May 2026 (Maresca replaced him); Slot left Liverpool (Iraola); Milner retired June 2026. Their figures are final.
Fee basis for transfer tabs
Signings and position tabs use the headline fee (guaranteed plus reported add-ons). Sales tabs use the guaranteed fee, because add-ons on older deals (Hazard, Coutinho) were never fully paid. Transfermarkt could not be read directly; fees vary between outlets, so each board should footnote its basis.
