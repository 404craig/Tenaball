# Keepers and discipline boards (Premier League, 1992/93 to end of 2025/26)

Main source for every board: the Premier League's own stats feed (the data behind premierleague.com stats pages), read directly on 3 October 2026 at
`https://footballapi.pulselive.com/football/stats/ranked/players/<stat>?comps=1` (all-time), with `&compSeasons=841` (2026/27) used to take off anything added since August 2026, `&compSeasons=777` (2025/26) to check season totals, and `&teams=<id>` for one-club figures. Because the live feed already includes the first weeks of 2026/27, every active player's 2026/27 figure was looked up and subtracted (details under each board). Cross-checked against The Analyst (Opta), Wikipedia "Premier League records and statistics" and the Premier League "Records: Players" page.

## Flags

- Clean sheets: the Premier League has Schwarzer 152 and Seaman 141; Opta (The Analyst) has 151 and 140. Rank order is the same either way. I used the Premier League figures. One ToffeeWeb article (September 2026) gave quite different figures for English keepers (James 166, Seaman 135, Martyn 131); no other source agrees, so I ignored it.
- The Wikipedia "List of Premier League goalkeepers with 100 or more clean sheets" page no longer exists (404 under both titles). I used the records page and the Premier League feed instead.
- Penalty saves: this board is put together from two Premier League sources (the 2018 full-era list for older keepers, the live feed from 2006/07 for later ones). StatMuse gives Fabianski 12, not 11. StatMuse's data doesn't cover whole careers (it has Gomes on 3 and Foster on 6), but it may still have one more save. I used the Premier League's 11, which fits the Premier League's 2022 tweet. Either way he is 3rd or joint 2nd, and the top 10 names are the same. The 8s below the cut-off may not be the full list: the 2018 list stopped at 10 names, and Tim Howard's saves before 2006 are not known (his total is 7 or 8).
- Red cards: sources disagree on who has 6. Planet Football (2022) lists Tugay and "Nemanja Matic" (probably a mistake for Vidic), and The Analyst leaves out Konchesky. The Premier League feed has 13 players on 6, Konchesky included, and Tugay on 5. I used the Premier League feed. Slots 8 to 10 need a pool of 13 names.
- Yellow cards: StatMuse and some betting-site lists give higher figures (Barry 129, Rooney 104, Young 95, Cattermole 95), probably counting second yellows differently. I used the Premier League feed, which matches the Premier League records page (Barry 123).
- 4-slot rule: in every board each answer is one person and fills one slot, so ties don't break it. The red cards board needs a 13-name pool for slots 8 to 10, and yellow cards needs Boateng and Nolan sharing 10th.
