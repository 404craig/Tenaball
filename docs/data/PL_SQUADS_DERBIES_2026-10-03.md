# Squads and derbies research (boards for Tenaball)

Compiled 3 October 2026. Period: Premier League 1992/93 to the end of 2025/26. Names are given the way fans use them, without accents.

How the "played in the Premier League" lists were made: every winning squad was taken from Wikipedia's tournament squad page, then matched by name against the game's own dataset (`docs/data/pl_players/pl_players.csv`, every PL player 1992/93 to 2025/26). Every match and every near miss (same surname) was then checked by hand. Name clashes (a different player with the same name) are listed under each squad as "Checked and excluded". The PL clubs, seasons and appearances in the tables come from the game's dataset, so the game can rank them directly.

## Flags

World Cup and Euros
- Name clashes removed (not the same person as the PL player of that name): Pele and Edu (Brazil 1970); Jorginho, Ricardo Rocha and Cafu (Brazil 1994); Cafu and Denilson (Brazil 2002); Eduardo (Portugal 2016, a goalkeeper who was at Chelsea but never played a PL game).
- Added by hand: Vasilios Lakis (Greece 2004), spelt Vassilis Lakis in the dataset (Crystal Palace 2004/05).
- Spain 2026 squad: Aymeric Laporte counts as a Spain player (switched from France). The squad is the 26 named by Wikipedia; Dean Huijsen and Dani Carvajal were left out.
- Gianluigi Donnarumma (Italy 2020) and Martin Zubimendi, Yeremy Pino (Spain 2026) only reach the list through 2025/26 seasons in the dataset; correct as of the end of 2025/26.
- The game spells some differently from Wikipedia: Juninho Paulista is Juninho, Juliano Belletti is Belletti, Theodoros Zagorakis is Theo Zagorakis, Emerson Palmieri is Emerson. Match on the game's spelling.
- 2014 Germany: Per Mertesacker, Mesut Ozil and the others are matched; no doubts.

Premier League and Champions League
- Wikipedia credits these with a Champions League medal though they were not at the club when it was won, or did not play: Joao Cancelo (2022/23, loaned to Bayern in January 2023 but credited as part of City's treble squad), Jonny Evans (2007/08, played early in the campaign then went on loan to Sunderland in January 2008), Caoimhin Kelleher (2018/19, unused reserve keeper), Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain (2018/19, injured almost all season), Kepa Arrizabalaga (2020/21 back-up keeper). I kept them, as Wikipedia lists them. Drop them if Craig wants only players who played in the campaign.
- Henrik Larsson and Gerard Pique are on Wikipedia's PL medal list with fewer than 10 PL games (7 and 9); kept, as the list includes them.
- Left out on purpose: Patrick Vieira (played for Inter in 2009/10 but left in January 2010, not credited), Jordi Cruyff (played three times in the 1998/99 campaign but was on loan at Celta Vigo by the final, not credited), Danny Simpson (played for Man Utd in the 2007/08 campaign, not credited and not a PL winner with them), Leroy Sane (joined Bayern after the 2020 final tournament had begun, not credited), Martin Odegaard (no Champions League win credited for his Real Madrid years).
- Wikipedia's medal list is the only source used for who has a PL medal; the game's dataset was not used to judge medals.
- Every name in sections 3 and 4 matches a name in the game's PL dataset once accents are removed (Wikipedia's "Mikel John Obi" is written here as the dataset's John Obi Mikel).
