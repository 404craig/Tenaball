## Flags

1. Bruno Fernandes (the one real doubt). Statbunker 27 and StatMuse 27 are current totals that include 2026/27. StatMuse's season split: 2019/20 4, 2020/21 9, 2021/22 0, 2022/23 2, 2023/24 4, 2024/25 3, 2025/26 4, 2026/27 1 (Ipswich, 30 Aug 2026). That gives 26 at the end of 2025/26.
   Problems: StatMuse's 2025/26 match list (Wolves 8 Dec, Bournemouth 15 Dec, Palace 1 Mar, Bournemouth 20 Mar) wrongly includes the 15 Dec 2025 goal v Bournemouth, which premierleague.com describes as a free kick, and leaves out his 97th-minute penalty winner v Burnley on 30 Aug 2025 (theScore, manutd.com). Those two mistakes cancel, so 4 in 2025/26 still looks right (Burnley, Wolves, Palace, Bournemouth away), but other gaps can't be ruled out. FootballFanCast (Nov 2024) said 23, which doesn't fit StatMuse's split (19 at that point plus at most one). The 2025/26 count was checked against match reports; earlier seasons were not.
   Used 26. If it were 27 he would be level with Aguero and Vardy in joint 7th; the ten names on the board are the same either way.
2. Gerrard 31 v 32 (see table). Used 31.
3. Shearer taken: 67 (The Analyst) v 65 (Statbunker). Scored is 56 everywhere.
4. Henry missed: 0 (FootballFanCast, Tales from the Top Flight) v 2 of 25 (The Analyst). Rooney missed: 11 (The Analyst, FootballFanCast, GiveMeSport) v 8 (Statbunker, which seems to count only kicks that were saved or missed and not ones hitting the woodwork). Used The Analyst for taken/missed where it has the player.
5. Taken and missed for Lampard, Salah, Noble, Aguero, Vardy and Gerrard are from Statbunker (or GiveMeSport for Gerrard), often a single source. Treat taken/missed as background only; the board stat should be penalties scored.
6. Single-source figures: Milivojevic 22 and Unsworth 22 (Statbunker only).
