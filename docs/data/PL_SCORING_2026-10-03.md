## Flags

- Streaks: ESPN (Dec 2025) said Calvert-Lewin could become "the 15th player" to score in seven in a row, but the Premier League table already has 15 different players at 7+. I used the Premier League table. Isak's run end date is 15 Jan 2025 in the PL table and 18 Jan 2025 at The Analyst; length (8) agrees. No 7+ run between January and May 2026 was found in searches, and the Aug 2026 Vanguard list adds none.
- Minutes: StatMuse is a single detailed source; Barry varies 54,429 to 54,439 between outlets. Milner shows 40,595 in an older StatMuse snapshot and 40,913 now (he retired after 2025/26, so 40,913 should be final); he is 12th either way unless the old figure is right, in which case he is 13th behind Distin. StatMuse's "appearances" column on that page is actually starts; I used real appearance totals.
- Sub goals: one detailed source (The Analyst, 4 Aug 2026), but Defoe 24 / Giroud 21 / Hernandez 19 / Crouch 16 agree with bet365 and older lists. Welbeck (active in 2025/26 at Brighton) is the only top-ten name that can still move.
- Headers: no single up-to-date full top ten. William Hill's "end of 2025/26" list leaves out Sheringham (36) and has Ferguson on 35 and Wood in tenth, so it is not reliable on its own; I used Opta's 2021 list updated. Ferguson 36 (Opta) vs 35 (William Hill): chose 36, which also ties him with Sheringham. Chris Wood: 29 (GiveMeSport Oct 2025, William Hill end of 2025/26) vs a Squawka figure implying 31 (in 2025/26). Either way he is outside the top ten (Giroud 32 is 10th).
- Free kicks: solid, The Analyst dated 14 Aug 2026.
- Non-penalty goals: Andrew Cole's single penalty is from StatMuse and agrees with Planet Football (Ferdinand "the only player in this top 10" never to score one). Shearer 204 (56 pens, Premier League official) vs 205 in Planet Football (55 pens): chose 204.
- Different clubs: one source (BBC Sport, Sep 2026). BBC gives Lampard 178 goals; the Premier League says 177; doesn't affect the club count. Guinness (2014) had Defoe on 36 then; he added a 37th later. No names below 34 found.
- Calendar year: incomplete, only 9 different players verified. 2025 calendar year not verified from any published figure.
- Outside the box: Hasselbaink 33 (GiveMeSport 2025, Football Faithful 2018) vs 32 (Pulse Sports 2025): chose 33. De Bruyne 30 (GiveMeSport, 12 Apr 2025, likely after his 12 Apr goal v Palace) vs 29 (Pulse Sports, 8 Apr 2025): chose 30; he may have added another before May 2025, unverified. Ranks 6 to 8 could shift by one.
