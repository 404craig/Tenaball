# SPFL lists: managers, title winners, captains, PFA awards, cups, promotion and relegation, final line-ups

Researched 5 October 2026. Figures and events stop at the end of the 2025/26 season (May 2026). Changes after that (June 2026 onwards) are listed only where they close a spell, and are marked "after cut-off".

Status key: **OK** = two sources agree. **CHECK** = sources disagree or only one source found; see the note.

Main sources used throughout:

- Wikipedia: club manager lists (Celtic, Rangers, Aberdeen, Hearts), each season's top-flight and second-tier article (managerial changes, infobox, play-off brackets), List of Scottish Cup finals, List of Scottish League Cup finals, PFA Scotland award pages, the three final articles.
- FitbaStats (fitbastats.com, `manager_timeline.php`, `manager.php`, `game.php` per club): seasons and games for every manager spell, and the line-ups of the three finals.
- RSSSF Scotland season pages (`rsssf.org/tabless/scotNN.html` and `scotYYYY.html`): final tables with champion, promotion, relegation and play-off notes, and every cup final score.
- AFC Heritage Trust (afcheritage.org/football/managers): Aberdeen managers with start and finish dates and competitive games.
- Plus spot checks named in each section (BBC Sport, PFA Scotland, SPFL, UEFA.com, Celtic Wiki, Gersnet, Sky Sports, The Scotsman).

1. Celtic: Rodgers' second spell is 124 games (Wikipedia) or 123 (FitbaStats). The interim parts of Lennon's two spells aren't split out in either source.
2. Rangers: McCoist's last day is 15 December 2014 (resignation) or 21 December 2014 (left the role).

4. Hearts: the 2006/07 caretakers (Malofeyev, Riabovas) and the date Ivanauskas left (2 or 20 March 2007); McAvoy's 2023 role; game counts for Burley, McGlynn (2005), László, Jefferies (2nd), Levein (2nd), Naismith and Critchley.
