# Cross-check of Transfermarkt club figures, Aberdeen, Hearts, Motherwell and Dundee United

Checked on 5 October 2026 against sources other than Transfermarkt. Every figure counts Scottish top-flight league games only (starts plus substitute appearances), seasons 2000/01 to 2025/26, for that club. Cup games, play-offs and second-tier seasons are left out: Hearts 2014/15 and 2020/21 (both Championship seasons; 2020/21 was not on the list we were given, but Hearts were in the Championship that season and Transfermarkt's figures already leave it out), Dundee United 2016/17 to 2019/20 and 2023/24. Raw pages are cached in the session scratchpad (`spfl_raw/xcheck/`).

## Sources and method

- **Aberdeen:** AFC Heritage player pages (afcheritage.org), which give league apps, sub apps and goals for every season. Seasons before 2000/01 (Mackie, Winters, Russell Anderson, Chris Clark) and 2026/27 are left out.
- **Hearts:** londonhearts.com player pages, "Appearances by Season" (league games column) and "Goals by Season" (league column). Seasons 1998/99, 1999/00 (Pressley, Kirk), 2014/15 and 2020/21 are left out.
- **Motherwell and Dundee United:** the "Appearances and goals by club, season and competition" table on each player's English Wikipedia page, league columns. Most of these tables cite Soccerbase. Where Wikipedia has no season table (Corrigan, Kenneth, Daly), Soccerbase is used instead.
- **Soccerbase** (soccerbase.com): the career table on each player page gives one league total per spell. It can't be split by season, so it's only a straight check where the whole spell is inside the window and in the top flight. Soccerbase also has a page per player per season listing every match he played, and those were used to settle every season where the sources disagreed (column "Soccerbase season check"). Soccerbase season ids run from 130 for 2000/01 to 145 for 2015/16, then 149 for 2016/17 to 158 for 2025/26.
- "Agree" means our figure matches Transfermarkt's. Where they differ, the season or seasons behind the gap are named.

## Does the top ten change?

No board's top ten changes who is in it: the 11th name stays 11th on every list (Winters, Lewis, Driver, Shankland, Quinn and McIntyre), each still clear of 10th. Some places move:

- **Aberdeen apps:** McGinn (279) goes from 5th to 3rd, above Langfield and Hayes (278).
- **Aberdeen goals:** Miller (29) joins Considine and Hayes on 29.
- **Hearts goals:** Walker (30) drops below Hartley (31) to 5th.
- **Motherwell apps:** Clarkson (226 or 228) goes above Corrigan (224) into 4th; McDonald (191) goes just above O'Donnell (190).
- **Dundee United apps:** McCracken (177) is still 2nd, now 4 clear of Rankin; McIntyre (141) is still 11th.

- Clarkson's 2005/06 appearances stay open: Wikipedia says 32, Soccerbase 30, Transfermarkt 28.
