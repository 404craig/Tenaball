# Scottish clubs' transfer-fee records (checked 5 October 2026)

Research for possible Tenaball boards. Fees run to the end of the summer 2026 window (deadline 1 September 2026, plus Rangers' Kevin Kelsy deal confirmed on 3 September 2026, which TM and the BBC both list in the summer window). Nothing here is in `index.html` yet.

## How the fees were checked

- **Source 1: Transfermarkt (TM).** Record pages, fees in euros: Celtic [sales](https://www.transfermarkt.co.uk/celtic-glasgow/rekordabgaenge/verein/371) and [signings](https://www.transfermarkt.co.uk/celtic-glasgow/transferrekorde/verein/371), Rangers [sales](https://www.transfermarkt.co.uk/glasgow-rangers/rekordabgaenge/verein/124) and [signings](https://www.transfermarkt.co.uk/glasgow-rangers/transferrekorde/verein/124), and the sales pages of Aberdeen (370), Hearts (43), Hibernian (903), Dundee United (1519), Motherwell (987), Kilmarnock (2553), Dundee (511), St Johnstone (2578), St Mirren (465), Falkirk (1191), Livingston (1241), Hamilton (2999), Ross County (2759), Inverness CT (2451), Dunfermline (1270) and Partick Thistle (2760). Note: TM's arrivals page is `transferrekorde`, not `rekordzugaenge` (that URL returns an error page). Dates come from TM's transfer history for each player.
- **Source 2: a pounds figure** from BBC Sport, Sky Sports, STV, the clubs, the Guardian, the Herald or Scotsman group, mostly reached through the Wikipedia tables [List of Celtic F.C. records and statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Celtic_F.C._records_and_statistics#Transfers), [List of Rangers F.C. records and statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Rangers_F.C._records_and_statistics#Transfers) (which takes every fee from BBC Sport and lists only the initial fee) and the season pages [List of Scottish football transfers summer 2007 to 2026 and winter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Scottish_football_transfers_summer_2026). Modern BBC, Sky and STV pages were read directly; old news.bbc.co.uk pages are blocked from this machine, so for pre-2010 deals the BBC figure is as quoted in the Wikipedia tables, backed by a web search of other reports.
- **Rule used for the pounds figure:** the fee reported at the time. Where a report splits an initial (guaranteed) fee from add-ons, the initial fee is used, as the Wikipedia Rangers table does, and the add-ons go in the notes. Ranking is by that pounds figure, not by TM's euros, because TM converts at the rate of the day and old deals look bigger in euros (for example Chris Sutton, £6m in 2000, is €9.00m on TM, while Auston Trusty, also £6m, is €7.10m).
- **Ties at the cut-off** are marked as a pool (`=`), so the quiz can let any of the tied names fill the shared places.
- Ratings: **OK** = TM and a pounds source agree (allowing for exchange rate). **Check** = sources disagree or the fee was undisclosed and only reported; the row stays but the note says what is shaky.

Raw pages are cached under the session scratchpad (`spfl_raw/transfers/`: TM pages, `hist/` transfer histories, `wiki/` Wikipedia wikitext, `bbc/` news pages).
