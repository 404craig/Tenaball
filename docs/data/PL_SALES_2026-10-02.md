# Biggest sales by Premier League clubs (headline fees, to 1 September 2026)

Basis: headline fee (guaranteed plus reported add-ons) as published by UK outlets, in pounds. Research was done through web search results that quote BBC Sport, Sky Sports, ESPN, The Athletic, Guardian, PA (via Irish/regional papers), theScore, beIN and Transfermarkt-based lists; direct page fetches were blocked. "Sky" means a Sky Sports article title or summary quoting the figure. Ranks share a number when fees are equal.

## Notes and uncertainties

- Loans with an obligation counted as sales at the combined fee: Hojlund (Man Utd to Napoli, 2025 loan, obligation triggered 2026, €50m/£43.2m) and Morata (Chelsea to Atletico, 2019 loan, permanent 2020, £58.3m). Kiwior (Arsenal to Porto, 2025 loan, obligation 2026) is about £19m to £22m and outside Arsenal's top ten. Harvey Elliott's 2025 loan to Aston Villa had a £35m obligation that was not triggered (he went on loan to Valencia in 2026), so he is not counted.
- Swaps left out: Henrikh Mkhitaryan (Man Utd to Arsenal, 2018) and Alexis Sanchez (Arsenal to Man Utd, 2018) moved in a straight swap with no fee; Transfermarkt values them at €34m and about £29m, which would put each in their club's top ten if counted.
- No player appears twice in any one list.
- Fee ranges that could change a placing: Oxlade-Chamberlain and Iwobi (some outlets say up to £40m); Kane (Sky "close to £100m", others "£100m+"); Isak (£125m or £130m); Walker (£50m or £54m); Nico Gonzalez (£50m or £52m); Minteh (£30m or £33m); Saint-Maximin (undisclosed, £23m to £30m); Spence (£25.6m or up to £30m); Sterling to Chelsea (£47.5m or c.£50m).
- Rashford: Barcelona did not take up the €30m option (June 2026). Reports linked him with Fenerbahce in August 2026 but no completed sale was found; Man Utd's summer income was put at about £45m, mostly Hojlund. If a late Turkish-window deal happened it was not found.
- Chelsea's 2026 figures (Jackson, Cucurella, Santos, Delap) come from PA, Sky and agency reports; they are recent and could be revised.
- Older fees (Overmars, Adebayor, Nasri, Beckham, Torres, Berbatov, Modric) are the commonly quoted UK figures; exact add-on totals were not separately verified.
