# Premier League relegations and promotions, 1992/93 to 2025/26

Method: counted from the game's own `PL` season tables (index.html line 1183). Relegated = bottom 3 (bottom 4 in 1994/95, when the league cut from 22 to 20). Promoted = a club in season N that was not in season N-1 (1993/94 to 2025/26). Then cross-checked against search-result text from the outlets named below. 2026/27 ignored (Coventry, Ipswich, Hull came up for it).

2025/26 check: Wolves relegated 20 April 2026 (theScore, The Canary), Burnley 22 April 2026 after 1-0 home loss to Man City (beIN Sports, Malay Mail: "fourth team to suffer relegation at least five times after Norwich, West Brom and Leicester"), West Ham on the final day, 24 May 2026, despite beating Leeds 3-0 (premierleague.com, ITV: "third time the Hammers have been relegated from the Premier League", after 2003 and 2011). Promoted for 2025/26: Leeds, Burnley, Sunderland (theplayoffs.news).

### Alternative: counting 1992 promotions into the inaugural season

Ipswich (Second Division champions), Middlesbrough (runners-up) and Blackburn (play-off winners) won promotion in 1991/92 and started in the first PL season (Wikipedia, 1992/93 FA Premier League). If those count:
- Middlesbrough 4 (1992/93, 1995/96, 1998/99, 2016/17), joining Palace, Watford, Fulham on 4. Top nine then fixed; the pool for slot 10 becomes the other eight on 3 (Newcastle, West Ham, Forest, Bolton, Birmingham, Wolves, Sheffield Utd, Hull) plus Ipswich.
- Ipswich 3 (1992/93, 2000/01, 2024/25), joins the 3 pool.
- Blackburn 2 (1992/93, 2001/02), no effect.
The Analyst (Opta) uses this convention: its current list shows Middlesbrough on 4 (and Ipswich and Hull on 4, which also includes their 2026/27 promotion, outside our scope). Another source (Wikipedia-based search text) gives Middlesbrough "4 promotions: 1991/92, 1994/95, 1997/98, 2015/16", also counting 1992.

## Disagreements and how settled
- Relegation 3-pool: brief said seven clubs, the tables give ten. Tables checked; West Ham (2003, 2011, 2026, ITV) and Wolves (2004, 2012, 2026) confirmed on 3, Forest confirmed on 3. Kept ten.
- Promotion 3-pool: brief said eight, tables give nine. The Analyst agrees on all except Hull and Middlesbrough, both explained by convention/2026 (Hull's 4th is 2026/27, out of scope; Boro's 4th is 1992). Kept nine.
- The Analyst's relegation summary did not name Palace among clubs on 4; other sources (HITC) and the tables give Palace 4. Kept 4.
