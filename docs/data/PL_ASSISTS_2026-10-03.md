# Assists boards (Premier League, 1992/93 to end of 2025/26)

Researched 3 October 2026. All figures stop at the final day of 2025/26 (24 May 2026). Anything a player added in 2026/27 has been taken off (see the notes).

How to read the sources: there are two families of assist figures.
- **Opta** (the Premier League's own data, used by premierleague.com, The Analyst, club sites and NBC). This is the official count and is used wherever a figure could be found.
- **StatMuse** (its own feed). Usually within 1 to 4 of Opta for older players, identical or within 1 for recent seasons. Used as the cross-check, and as the only source for the deeper parts of the club lists where no Opta list could be found. Those rows are marked "SM".

Goals for board 3 and board 7 were checked against Craig's own dataset (`docs/data/pl_players/pl_players.csv`), which matched every goals figure below.

## Flags

1. **De Bruyne 119 or 118.** The Analyst (Opta) says 119 total and 118 for City; premierleague.com says 118 and 117. Chose 119 / 118 (The Analyst, and NBC agrees). Doesn't change any ranking.
2. **2026/27 creep in later sources.** NBC's Sept 2026 list and StatMuse include early 2026/27 games. Taken off: Bruno 73 to 72, Robertson 61 to 60, Foden 36 to 34. Saka, Odegaard, Porro and Haaland had no 2026/27 assists by then. Milner (90) is only Opta via NBC; one search snippet showed a different, much lower total, which looks wrong. 10th place is safe whatever his exact figure.
3. **Board 2's 15-assist pool.** Nine players tie on 15 for places 9 and 10. Cantona 1992/93 is 16 in Goal and StatMuse but 15 in Football Faithful. If 15, he joins the pool (ten names for two places). Solano 1999/00 (15) is in Goal and StatMuse but not Football Faithful. Fabregas 2014/15 is 18 (one outlet says 19; ignored).
4. **Board 3:** Kane is 46 assists by Opta (259) and 47 by StatMuse (260, which ties Andy Cole for 6th). Chose Opta. Sheringham 222 or 221 (one outlet), still 10th either way.
5. **Club boards (board 4).** Opta-sourced: Man Utd 1 to 4, Liverpool 1 to 3, Arsenal 1 to 8, Chelsea 1 to 3 and 5, City 1 to 4, Spurs 1 to 10 (club chart). The rest come from StatMuse only, which runs 0 to 4 below Opta for older players, so the lower places for **Liverpool, Chelsea and Man City are single-source and shaky**. Pools at the cut: Liverpool 9th (Mane, Owen, Fowler on 30), Chelsea 10th (Mata, Gudjohnsen, Hasselbaink on 27), Spurs 10th (Bale, Kulusevski on 22, maybe Keane). Arsenal 9th: Van Persie 39 (StatMuse) or 40.
6. **Defender boards (5 and 6) depend on the definition.** StatMuse counts Antonio Valencia as a defender for his whole career, including 2011/12 (13 assists) when he was a right winger. The Analyst/Opta single-season record is "Alexander-Arnold 13 (2019/20)" with no mention of Valencia, which suggests Opta doesn't count that season. Recommend leaving Valencia off board 6, and either leaving him off board 5 or saying "players listed as defenders". Board 6 has an eight-way tie at 8 assists for 10th place, so it's better as a top 9 with a pool, or dropped. Ashley Cole is 31 (StatMuse) or 35 (some outlets).
7. **Board 7 borderline cases** sit exactly on 50: Fabregas, Young and Murphy (50 goals), and Barmby, Firmino, Sigurdsson, Yorke and Saka (50 assists, Opta via NBC/OptaJoe). StatMuse has some older players lower (for example Fabregas 108 assists, still fine). NBC's Sept 2026 list left out Saka even though OptaJoe confirms his 50, so another player sitting exactly on 50 could be missing too; no other candidate was found. Kane is left out (46 by Opta).
