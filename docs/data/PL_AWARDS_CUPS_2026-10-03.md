## Flags

- **No May 2026 monthly awards.** The brief asked for "the May 2026 award included", but neither Player nor Manager of the Month has had a May award since May 2021. Totals run to April 2026. Search found only club awards for May 2026 (Arsenal's, Man Utd's), not a Premier League one.
- **Player of the Month club totals count shared months twice** (once per player's club): Liverpool x2, Arsenal x1, Spurs x1, Blackburn x1, and Man Utd/Southampton once each for November 1997 (Cole and Davies, different clubs). If a board should count months rather than players, Liverpool drop to 35, Arsenal 31, Spurs 25, Blackburn 4; the top 10 order does not change.
- **Wikipedia's own date for the Gerrard/Suarez shared award is inconsistent** (March 2014 in one note, April 2014 in another). Does not affect counts.
- **Scott Parker, Young Player 2003/04**: he moved Charlton to Chelsea in January 2004; Wikipedia lists both clubs. Choose "Charlton Athletic" if one club must be shown (most of his season was there), or accept both.
- **Tied cut-offs**: Player of the Month players (9 tied on 4 for places 9 and 10), Golden Glove keepers (4 tied on 1 for places 8 to 10), Manager of the Season (9 tied on 1 for places 6 to 10). All need a pool.
- **4-slot rule on year boards (one row per season)**, for whoever builds them:
  - FA Cup winners: Arsenal or Chelsea hit 4 in many 10-season windows. Windows that pass: 1992/93-2001/02, 1993/94-2002/03, 1994/95-2003/04, 1998/99-2007/08, 1999/00-2008/09, 2000/01-2009/10, 2001/02-2010/11, 2007/08-2016/17, 2009/10-2018/19, 2014/15-2023/24, 2015/16-2024/25, 2016/17-2025/26. If beaten finalists are shown as answers too, note Chelsea lost 2017, 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2026 (5 in 2016/17-2025/26), which breaks the most recent window.
  - League Cup winners: every window from 2009/10-2018/19 onward fails (Man City has 4 to 6). Windows up to 2008/09-2017/18 all pass.
  - Award year boards with clubs as answers break the rule often (Man Utd and Man City); with players as answers they are all fine (no player has 4 in any 10 seasons). Manager of the Season by season breaks it (Ferguson, Guardiola), so use it as a count board.
- **PFA Players' Player 2025/26** was presented in August 2026 (the PFA's own page is dated 25 August 2026), after the season, but it is a 2025/26 award, so it is in.
- Everything else matched across the Wikipedia list, its own summary tables and the second source. Nothing here is single-source except the per-month detail before 2025/26, which rests on Wikipedia's premierleague.com citations.
