## Sources and method

- 2016/17 to 2025/26: per-match FPL data (github.com/vaastav/Fantasy-Premier-League, `data/<season>/gws/merged_gw.csv`), summing `goals_scored` for rows where the player played for Liverpool in that fixture (team column from 2020/21; before that, Liverpool fixtures identified via opponent_team id). All 38 gameweeks present for 2025/26 (last kickoff 2026-05-24). Own goals not credited to anyone.
- Earlier seasons: club and player career totals from search-result text (liverpoolfc.com, LFChistory.net, Wikipedia, 11v11, sporting-heroes), with season splits that sum to those totals. Where an individual season split was not seen in a source it is marked (m) = from memory, but the player's total is anchored on a sourced career figure.
