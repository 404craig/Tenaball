## Players on 60 or more at the end of 2025/26

Exactly six: Cristiano Ronaldo (140), Lionel Messi (129), Robert Lewandowski (109), Karim Benzema (90), Raul (71), Kylian Mbappe (70).

Haaland (57), Muller (57), van Nistelrooy (56) and Kane (54) were all below 60.

## 2025/26 Champions League top scorer

Confirmed: Kylian Mbappe, 15 goals in 11 appearances (13 in the league phase, a record for that stage). Harry Kane second with 14. Source: UEFA.com and realmadrid.com (31 May 2026).
